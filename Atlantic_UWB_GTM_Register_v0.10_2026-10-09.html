Atlantic UWB GTM: Assumptions and Decisions Register

9 Oct 2026 · JC (product and vertical owner)

About this register

This register lists every decision and assumption behind the Atlantic UWB go-to-market plan (deck v0.10, 7 Oct 2026) and the product sheets v0.1 (9 Oct 2026). JC built the framework as product and vertical owner, and stood in for several other roles to do it. Each item names the role that should own it, so that owner can confirm, change or reject it.

  • Stood in = who gave the input during the build: JC, the CIO documents, the product map, or Claude (an assumption proposed during drafting).
  • Should own = the role accountable for the final answer. Where JC is not that role, the item needs that owner's sign-off.
  • Status = Confirmed (owner agreed) · Ratify (decided in the build, owner must confirm) · To verify (needs data or a test) · Open (no decision yet).
  • Confidence (assumptions only) = Certain (primary source) · Likely (good secondary source or inference) · Guessing (judgement; never in a Sales Sheet).

Nothing here is final until its owner signs it. Until then, use the prices for pilots only.
