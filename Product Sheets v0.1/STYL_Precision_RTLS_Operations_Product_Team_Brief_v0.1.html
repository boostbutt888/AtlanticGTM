Why the price is correct

5 decisions. Each decision has a reason and a check.

Decision 1 — Price unit

  • Reason: operators budget by site and floor area. A price per tag puts us against BLE tags at US$2–6.
  • Reason: floor area sets the anchor count, our main hardware cost.
  • Check: the survey measures the area before the quote. Cargo needs many tags, so extra tags are a separate pack line.

Decision 2 — Three parts

  • Reason: we see the margin of each part. Hardware is a service, so the customer has no capital cost.
  • Check: the hardware margin is 76% at the capacity limit (decision 5).

Decision 5 — Value and hardware check

Value Guessing60 staff a shift, 3 shifts, 10 minutes saved a shift = 10,950 hours a year. At S$30 an hour: about S$329,000 a year. The cargo price is S$264,000 a year.
Hardware8 anchors × S$100 + 40 tags × S$40 = S$2,400 per 1,000 m². Over 60 months plus 10% spares: about S$60 a month. Price S$250: margin 76%. Capacity: 8 anchors × 8 tags = 64 tags at one time. Cargo has 150–225 tags per 1,000 m²E.
Margin and discount rules
No quote below 30% gross margin. Target 40% for each customer over 24 months. Sales Director: up to 20%. CEO: more than 20%. No discount on the minimum fee or the platform fee. Small site check: 2,000 m², cloud, minimum fee: margin about 40%.
