Why the price is correct

5 decisions. Each decision has a reason and a check.

Decision 1 — Price unit

  • Reason: operators budget by gate and by bus. The gate count sets the anchor count.
  • Check: the gate study counts lanes and doors before the quote.

Decision 2 — Three parts

  • Reason: set-up is large (1,210 anchors, 150 engineer-days), so it is a separate line.
  • Check: hardware margin 67% (decision 5).

Decision 3 — Packages and minimum

PackagePriceBuyerReason for the price
Transit, self-hosted START HERES$400 per gate a year + S$95,000; minimum S$250,000Transit authority or operatorFixed cost S$179,600 a year (self-hosted + 24/7). The minimum keeps a small first phase above 30%.

Decision 4 — Benchmarks

No public price found for UWB gates. Shenzhen Tong and NXP did not publish one Certain. The rule asks for two public prices. Action: search GeBIZ and LTA awards, and ask NXP for gate prices.

Decision 5 — Value and hardware check

Value Guessing10 stations × 30,000 riders a day × 2 seconds saved = 167 hours a day, 61,000 hours a year. At S$15 an hour: about S$0.9M a year. The price is S$642,000 a year.
Hardware2 hardened anchors × S$200 = S$400 per gate: about S$10 a month with spares. Price S$30: margin 67%. Capacity: 2 anchors × 8 = 16 phones at one time per lane. A lane at 40 riders a minuteE.
Margin and discount rules
No quote below 30% gross margin. Target 40% for each customer over 24 months. Sales Director: up to 20%. CEO: more than 20%. No discount on the minimum fee or the platform fee. The gate study is not credited.
