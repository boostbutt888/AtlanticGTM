Why the price is correct

5 decisions. Each decision has a reason and a check.

Decision 1 — Price unit

  • Reason: airports budget by terminal and zone. Public area sets the anchor count.
  • Check: the survey measures the public area before the quote.

Decision 2 — Three parts

  • Reason: we see the margin of each part. The 24/7 support (cost S$93,000 a year) is in the software price.
  • Check: hardware margin 76% (decision 5).

Decision 3 — Packages

PackagePriceBuyerReason for the price
Terminal, self-hosted START HERES$12,000 per 1,000 m² a year + S$95,000Terminal operations headCAG requires its own data centre. Fixed cost S$179,600 a year (self-hosted + 24/7).

Decision 4 — Benchmarks

No public price found. Xovis, Veovo, SITA and Amadeus do not publish prices Certain. The rule asks for two public prices. Action: find two prices in GeBIZ awards or partner quotes before release.

Decision 5 — Value and hardware check

Value Guessing2 million departing passengers a year through the pier. If Flow moves 2 minutes from queue to shops, at S$0.50 spend a minute: about S$2M a year in retail. The price is S$470,000 a year.
Hardware8 anchors × S$100 + 40 tags × S$40 = S$2,400 per 1,000 m²: about S$60 a month with spares. Price S$250: margin 76%. Capacity: 64 tags at one time covers 40 staff tags. A busy queue has up to 500 people per 1,000 m²E.
Margin and discount rules
No quote below 30% gross margin. Target 40% for each customer over 24 months. Sales Director: up to 20%. CEO: more than 20%. No discount on the minimum fee or the platform fee. The 24/7 support share is the largest cost.
